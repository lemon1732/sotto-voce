// How it works

Local first.
Smart when you need it.

Two-stage pipeline — fast on-device recognition, then optional AI polish. Your audio never leaves your Mac.

01

Press your hotkey, speak

Hold your custom hotkey and talk naturally. Sotto detects speech with a local VAD model — no round-trips, instant activation.

02

Whisper transcribes on-device

Apple Silicon's Neural Engine runs WhisperKit locally. Your audio never leaves your Mac. Results in ~300ms after you stop speaking.

03

AI cleans it up

One quick call to your own API key (Claude, GPT-4o, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint) removes filler words and reformats for context.

04

Text appears at your cursor

Polished text is injected into any app — Slack, Notes, Terminal, your IDE. Release the hotkey and keep typing if you want.

// FAQ

Common questions.

Does it work without the internet?
The speech recognition part is fully offline — WhisperKit runs locally on Apple Silicon. The AI cleanup step needs internet to call your API key, but you can disable it and use pure local transcription whenever you're offline.
What is BYOK and do I need it?
"Bring Your Own Key" means you sign up directly with an AI provider (Anthropic for Claude, OpenAI for GPT-4o, etc.) and paste your API key into Sotto. You pay them directly — not us — at roughly $0.0001 per cleanup call. If you don't want AI cleanup at all, you can use Sotto as a pure on-device transcription tool. No key required.
How does the AI cleanup differ from regular transcription?
Raw transcription captures exactly what you said — including "um", "uh", "like", mid-sentence corrections, and repetitions. The AI cleanup step sends the raw transcript to your chosen LLM with context about which app you're in, and gets back a polished version that reads like you typed it carefully. The cleanup usually takes 0.5–1 second.
Will my voice audio ever leave my Mac?
Never. Only the final transcript text is sent to your LLM provider for cleanup — and only if you have BYOK enabled. The raw audio is processed entirely on-device by WhisperKit and is never stored or transmitted anywhere.
What Mac hardware do I need?
Sotto requires a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later). This is what makes fast local inference possible. Intel Macs are not supported.
What happens to pricing after early access?
